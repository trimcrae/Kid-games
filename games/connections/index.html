How do you play?

Sixteen cards hide four secret groups of four. Tap four cards you think belong together, then press Check ✓. Get it right and the group locks in — and tells you why those four go together.

  • Careful: some cards look like they fit two groups. Only one is right!
  • Stuck? 💡 Hint tells you a group's name — it never costs a guess.
  • Trying the same four cards twice is free too.
  • Every level has a fair number of guesses: 🧸 Tiny gets 6, 🌱 Easy gets 5, the rest get 4.

Keyboard: arrow keys move · Space picks · Enter checks · S shuffles · C clears · H hints · Esc goes back
