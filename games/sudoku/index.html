How to play

Fill each row, column and thick-bordered box with every number once. The dark numbers are clues and cannot change.

Pick a square, then tap a number. Turn on Notes to pencil in possibilities. A ! means a number repeats in its row, column or box.

Little grid is a good place to begin. Gentle and Classic can be solved one logical step at a time. Challenge has fewer clues for a longer think.

Keyboard: Arrow keys move around the grid. Type a number to enter it; Backspace or Delete erases. N switches notes. Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes your last move.
