🧠 How does converting units work?

Every measurement can be written in lots of different units. 3 feet, 1 yard, and 0.9144 metres are all the same length — just different rulers!

The rule: going from a big unit to a small one, you get more of them, so you multiply. Going from a small unit to a big one you get fewer, so you divide. 2 m → cm is 2 × 100 = 200 cm. 250 cm → m is 250 ÷ 100 = 2.5 m.

The green × and ÷ under each unit below tells you exactly which one to do. 🔢

SI units (metres, kilograms, litres) are used by most of the world and by all scientists. Imperial units (feet, pounds, gallons) are used in the USA. Being able to switch between them is a superpower! 💪
