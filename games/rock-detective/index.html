🧠 The three rock families & the rock cycle

Igneous rocks form when hot melted rock (magma or lava) cools and hardens — like granite and basalt. 🌋

Sedimentary rocks form when bits of sand, mud, or shells pile up in layers and get squished together over ages — like sandstone and limestone. They often hold fossils! 🐚

Metamorphic rocks are other rocks that got changed by heat and pressure deep underground — limestone becomes marble, shale becomes slate. 🔥

In the rock cycle, rocks slowly turn into each other over millions of years — melting, wearing down, and squishing round and round forever. ♻️
