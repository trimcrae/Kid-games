Your island, your controls

Mouse / touch: drag the world to turn it. Tap a square to build; switch to Mine to remove a block. Tap the supply rocks to gather.

Keyboard: focus the island. Arrow keys move the square; Page Up / Page Down change layer. Space builds, M mines, and 1–3 select a block. Q / E turn the camera; + / − zoom.

Map: tap any coordinate button to select it, then use Place. You can also type Across, Along, and Layer directly. Blocks need support, and towers are mined from the top.

Every mission asks you to make the math with blocks. There are no timers, scores, or lost lives.
